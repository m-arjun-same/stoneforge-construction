STONEFORGE® · Bengaluru — Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions clients ask us most. Everything here comes from the Forge itself; the binding details are always in your written quote.

How do I book a consultation?

Start with a pre-build discussion — an essential first step, usually on phone or WhatsApp. Samples and past projects are shown at the Forge Yard. The consultation fee is ₹5,000, adjusted against your project total. It is fully refunded if you cancel before 72 hours of the consultation; within 72 hours it is non-refundable if cancelled or postponed.

How can I pay?

Cash, Net Banking (RTGS / NEFT / IMPS), Wallet (Paytm), UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay) or Cheque. There is no debit/credit card machine. Payment is due on the day of the consultation.

Can I visit the Forge Yard?

By prior appointment only — no walk-ins. Call any day between 10:30 AM and 7 PM on +91 80471 22650. The Forge Yard is at Plot 42, Forge Yard, Whitefield Industrial Area, Bengaluru 560066.

Do you publish project prices?

Pricing is transparent and ready — contact the Forge on WhatsApp, call or email and the complete rate card is shared instantly. Additional drawing revisions beyond your project scope are ₹600 each. Private projects (sites not published online) carry a non-usage fee of 40% on project value.

What should we know before we come?

No personal photography or videos are allowed inside active Forge sites. Safety helmets are mandatory on site — please carry closed shoes; visitors are given a helmet and vest at the gate. STONEFORGE does not offer demolition-only services.

When do we receive our drawings?

Draft drawings are ready 1 week after the consultation / invoice closing. Final drawings are delivered within 3 weeks of your approval, via a cloud link sent by email (about 30 days to download — a computer is recommended, as the files are large). "Rush my Order" delivers in 10 days, excluding prints and models. Prints are ready in 1 week; physical models take up to 4 weeks (domestic) or 10 weeks (international). Client data is stored for 90 days from the consultation.

What detailing is included?

Structural detailing, load calculations and basic finish schedules. The Forge does not offer unapproved structural changes, removing load-bearing elements, or adding floors beyond sanction.

Do the structures carry a mark?

All finished structures carry the STONEFORGE mark cast in one of the four entrance corners (the position is not choosable); unfinished structures have no mark. Mark-free structures can be requested through the client form option. Prints are held for 2 weeks only, then discarded without refund — Forge Yard pickup is preferred.

Who owns the drawings?

Under the Indian Copyright Act 1957, copyright stays with the architect; clients may buy the copyright through the client form. The mark may not be removed or covered. For Forge-built projects, STONEFORGE may share images online unless you opt out in the booking form notes.

When is the best time to start a build?

Right after the dry season begins — book 2–3 weeks ahead. Each floor cycle takes 30–45 days. Materials and a wide equipment range are provided (usage per project). Interior styling is not included, and outside contractors are not allowed on Forge sites. Family members are welcome at milestone visits. Outdoor works happen at sunrise or in the hour before sunset; site sessions run 11 AM – 8 PM, with no weekend surcharge. Surface blemishes are corrected at snagging; your progress link arrives about a week after each pour and final handover takes 3–4 weeks after the last finish.

Where can we build?

All projects include a Forge Yard consultation at no extra charge. Preferred zones: Whitefield, Sarjapur Road, Devanahalli and Hebbal. Travel and municipal charges are extra, paid by the client at current rates.

What equipment does the Forge use?

Two Liebherr tower cranes, a Putzmeister concrete pump, Bosch total stations and a fleet of JCB excavators, with Sika waterproofing systems and UltraTech structural concrete. The equipment is not for sale or rent.
